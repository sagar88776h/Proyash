# Content, photos and stories — guide for publishers

PRAYASH is a real organisation. Everything on the public website must be true,
confirmed and respectful of the people PRAYASH works with. The website was
built **without inventing** any history, registration details, numbers of
members, villages, beneficiaries, donations or achievements. Where those belong,
it shows a clearly labelled placeholder (“To be provided by PRAYASH”) until
someone with the right role fills it in.

## Where each kind of content is edited

| Content | Where | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page text (home, about, request help, support us, volunteer, transparency, contact, privacy, terms…) | Website → Page content | Publishers, administrators |
| Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, map link | Website → Settings → Contact details | Administrators |
| UPI ID, bank details, donation instructions | Website → Settings → Donation payment details | Administrators |
| Registered name, registration details, year established | Website → Settings → Organisation details | Administrators |
| Social media links | Website → Settings → Social media | Administrators |
| Villages | Programmes → Villages | Publishers, administrators |
| Projects, outcomes, budgets, volunteers | Programmes → Projects | Publishers publish; coordinators may draft |
| Photographs | Programmes → Photo library | Publishers and coordinators upload; publishers publish |
| Financial reports | Finance → Reports | Treasurers and administrators |

The portal overview shows a **Launch checklist** that tracks the most important
of these (contact details, payment details, reviewed policies, story, photos).

## Page content

- Edits are saved as a **draft**. Nothing changes on the website until you
  choose **Save and publish**.
- Each publication creates a numbered **version**. Earlier versions can be
  loaded back into the editor from the history panel.
- **Revert website to default text** removes the published version so the page
  shows its built-in text again.
- Default text only restates PRAYASH’s name, tagline, mission and brand
  message, and explains how the website works. Anything about PRAYASH’s
  history (“Our story”) is empty until PRAYASH writes it.
- The **privacy policy and terms** are drafts written for this website. They
  are shown with a “draft” notice until a reviewed version is published. Have
  them checked by someone qualified (including against India’s Digital Personal
  Data Protection Act, 2023) and fill in the “Last updated” date.

Writing tips: short sentences, plain words, no promises that help will be
given, and no figures that have not been checked against records.

## Photographs

### Before uploading

- Use **authentic PRAYASH photographs** only. No stock images presented as
  PRAYASH’s work.
- When uploading, the portal resizes the image and **removes location (GPS)
  and camera data** before it leaves your device.

### Before publishing

The database will refuse to publish a photo unless:

1. it has a short **description (alt text)** for people using screen readers —
   describe what is shown, e.g. “Volunteers sorting rice bags in the community
   hall”; and
2. you have recorded whether **identifiable people** appear in it; and
3. if they do, **informed consent is recorded as obtained**.

Informed consent means the person (or a parent or guardian for a child)
understood that the photo will appear publicly on the website and agreed. Note
in the consent field how and when it was given. Be especially careful with:

- children — prefer photos where children cannot be identified;
- people receiving help — never publish a photo that reveals someone is
  receiving assistance unless they have clearly agreed to that;
- medical situations, homes and documents.

If consent is withdrawn, **unpublish** the photo immediately: the public copy is
deleted.

## Projects and outcomes

- Projects start as drafts. Only **publishers or administrators** can publish
  a project or edit one that is already published.
- **Outcomes** (e.g. “120 notebooks distributed”) appear publicly only when
  they are marked **verified** — confirm the figure against distribution lists,
  receipts or attendance records first. Unverified outcomes stay internal.
- A project’s **budget** is visible only to finance roles. The public sees a
  project’s verified spending, calculated from the ledger, once the project is
  published.
- **Stories about people helped** must not identify them (name, photo, address,
  specific circumstances) unless they gave informed consent to that specific
  story being published. When in doubt, describe the activity, not the person.

## Volunteers

Volunteer names are never shown publicly unless the volunteer ticked the
public-recognition box when registering. Do not mention volunteers by name in
page content or project descriptions without their agreement.
